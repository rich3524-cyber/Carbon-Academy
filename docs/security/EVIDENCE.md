| EX-02 | 02 (ASVS 6.3.3, 6.5.2, 6.6.2, 7.5.2; NIST SP 800-63B email) | Applies to the **learner** role only; admin accounts must use MFA (control 03, Phase 6). Learners sign in with an email magic link only:<br>• a single factor, sent by email, which ASVS 5.0 and NIST SP 800-63B treat as an unsafe out-of-band channel (6.3.3; the 7.1.1 deviation)<br>• a one-time code stored as a fast hash of the email and code, not a salted slow hash (6.5.2)<br>• links that work in any browser, not just the one that asked (6.6.2)<br>• learners can end all their sessions but can't see a list of them (7.5.2) | PRD 6.3 requires magic-link-only sign-in. Learners are retail staff and customers, often on shared store devices, and hold low-sensitivity data: name, email, store and training progress. Passwords would bring credential stuffing, reuse and reset risks for little benefit. Tying a link to one browser (PKCE) would break the usual case: asking on the store device and opening the email on a phone | Beyond what ASVS Level 2 already requires anyway:<br>• `otp_length = 10`, the maximum (about 33 bits, against ASVS's 20-bit floor)<br>• Supabase limits that also cover direct API calls: `token_verifications` 10 per 5 minutes per IP, 60 seconds between links per address, and the hourly email cap<br>• a confirm button, so nothing signs in without the learner's tap<br>• no password can ever be set on an account (`refuse_password_change` trigger)<br>• email changes need both mailboxes to confirm, and send a notice<br>• "Signed in as <email>" on the dashboard, so the wrong account is noticed<br>• no sensitive learner actions: no exports, payments or admin functions<br>• certificates verified separately (Phase 5) | Re-review at the Phase 7 release gate, and in any case by **29 October 2027**. Re-review sooner if learners gain any higher-risk feature. Revisit if Supabase adds a cross-device-safe binding or passkeys | Rich, Sales and Marketing Manager, in chat, 29 October 2026 |
